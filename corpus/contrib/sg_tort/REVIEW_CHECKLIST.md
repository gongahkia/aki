# Review Checklist

Reviewer must confirm:

- Authorship certification is present.
- No real exam, tutorial, paid, private, or account-gated text is copied.
- `topics` are canonical SG Tort topics.
- `fact_pattern` is distinct from `model_answer`.
- `issues_expected` or `model_answer` is present.
- `answer_visibility` is `hidden`.
- Singapore law fit is plausible.
- The record tests at least one concrete issue, not only a generic rule.
- Any sensitive personal data has been removed.
- `source.source_id` exists in `corpus/source_registry.json`.
